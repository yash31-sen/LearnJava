Real Numbers:

numbers that can be written in number line.

eg: 5,-8, 0, -123, root(5/4), root(6/7) etc.

Real numbers are of 2 types:

1)Rational  numbers:numbers that can be written in the form of p/q,  7/1,-8/1,root(16), root(25)

\-> rational numbers are of 3 types:
a)Integers: p/q where p=1, -1 ------infinite. 

b)Fraction:p/q where q!==

c)Terminating Decimal: 

composite numbers: having more than 2 factors.

not prime then composite

1 is neither prime nor composite

Co-prime: 2 numbers having HCF 1, (7,13), (19,23)

Twin-Prime: 2 prime values when their difference is 2 

(11,13), (17,19), (5,7), (29,31).

Perfect Number: a number is perfect is the sum of their factor(excluding the number) is equal to the number.



2)Irrational numbers: can't be written in the form of p/q, root(2), root(3), pie=3.146...

Imaginary numbers: number that can't be written in number line.

root(-7), root(-1), root(-25).

for imaginary numbers i^2 =1;
